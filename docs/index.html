AgentGraph documentation

AgentGraph indexes the work you already do in Slack, Discord, Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, and Gmail into a local graph that humans and AI clients can query through the CLI, the web viewer, or MCP.

Quick path: uv sync --extra all, agentgraph onboard, agentgraph serve, then connect your assistant with agentgraph mcp-config.

What AgentGraph does

  • Connectors: Pull messages, documents, spreadsheets, folders, and email threads into normalized graph entities.
  • Browser observation: Watches supported URLs and triggers fetches when you dwell on a page long enough for it to be meaningful.
  • Local graph query: Supports search, structured filters, graph traversal, and targeted re-fetches over one local store.
  • Assistant access: Exposes the same graph to CLI users and AI clients through MCP.
